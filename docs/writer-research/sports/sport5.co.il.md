## Article types on this site
- **News / yedi'a (מערכת):** Desk byline `מערכת אתר ערוץ הספורט`. Headline is a fact or a quote. First sentence does the news. Short; logistics or a CV graf at the end. Recognize: no "אני", no section heads, often a video dek starting `צפו:`.
- **Match report / ידיעת משחק:** Result + `(יום)` in sentence one; score in Israeli `lost:won` order when someone "ניצחה"; nicknames; minute codes; a hero graf; look-ahead closer. On big Israeli games the same story is also a live `playbyplay` blog with `שריקת פתיחה` / minute lines / caption jokes.
- **Locker-room / post-match quotes:** Hed is a quote. One wrap graf, then `שם תפקיד:` quote blocks. Player gets סיפר. Stops when the quotes stop.
- **Interview / ראיון:** Named writer + show name in the dek. Status lede, then Q/A with the question as a bare line. Close = last answer + `האזינו:`.
- **Transfer explainer:** `עושים סדר` / sectioned body (money, timetable, eligibility, fit). Scoops tagged `כפי שפורסם לראשונה בערוץ הספורט`.
- **Analysis / ניתוח:** Dek says ניתוח or asks a craft question. Pivot question in the body. Numbers + role words. Forecast ending.
- **Column / פרשנות / טור אוהד:** Kicker `פרשנות:` in the hed, or dek `אוהד ירוק` / `טור דעה`. First person, argument lede, demand or next-event close.
- **Feature / magazine slot:** Series name (`מעקב הליגיונרים`), wordplay hed, labelled sub-profiles, more room for thesis than a yedi'a.

## Recurring structure
- **Chrome:** H1 hed + H2 dek (standfirst) + byline + `DD.MM.YY - HH:MM`. The dek is doing real work — score, quote, or thesis — not a SEO leftover.
- **Lede rules:** News and transfers open on a two-to-four-word drumroll (`זה קורה:`, `חזר ובגדול.`, `איזה משחק מטורף.`, `ההעברה הגדולה של הקיץ.`) then the fact. Match reports put winner, `(יום)`, score, and stake in sentence one. Columns open on a question, a false narrative, or a scene of the occasion. Dateline is the parenthetical day, not a city slug.
- **Score in the first breath:** Yes for match wraps. Injury/news briefs may bury the 1:0 under a human hed but still dump the result in graf 1.
- **Paragraph rhythm:** Desk yedi'ot = 2–4 fat grafs. Explainers = labelled chunks. Interviews = short Q, long A. Match recaps = lede / heroes / scene / other locker room / next fixture. Analysis = claim, evidence, hedge, forecast.
- **Quotes:** Arrival and post-match pieces stack `X אמר:` immediately after one scene line. Interviews keep the writer out of the answers. Match recaps often run quote-light and spend the space on nicknames and history. Analysis quotes, if any, arrive late as a single supporting line.
- **Endings:** News → next practical step (MRI, medicals, presentation hour). Match report → next opponent / Europe / "עוד פרק ביריבות". Column → demand, greeting, or next race/week. Interview → subject's last sentence. Do not moralize in desk voice; leave that to signed columns.

- כבש / הבקיע / השווה / קבע את תוצאת המשחק / ספגה / הדף / נגח / הפציץ מקרוב / שלח כדור רוחב / ניצלה מצבים נייחים

**Transfer / news verbs**
- נחת, סיכם באופן עקרוני, הגיעו להסכמה, משיכת חבל, בדיקות רפואיות, יוצג בפני, כפי שפורסם לראשונה, לפי / באתלטיק מדווחים / ע"פ

**Attribution**
- Desk: `X אמר:` / `Y המשיך:` / `סיפר:` (player) / colon after `מאמן…`
- Rare in these samples: טען, הודה, לדבריו, מסר — the house prefers אמר / סיפר / the Q itself
- Analysis/column: לדעתי, בלטו לי, כפי שפרסמתי לראשונה

**Analysis diction**
- עומק, היררכיה, תבניות התקפה, פליימייקר, מסירות מפתח, אחד על אחד, נשכבָה לאחור, מחץ התקפי, כמו כפפה ליד, לעשות סדר

## What a personal-newspaper writer should steal
1. **Write the dek as a second hed.** One line that already has the score, the quote, or the thesis. Haiton items should not drop a naked headline.
2. **Drumroll, then fact.** Two words, colon or period (`זה קורה:`, `חזר ובגדול.`), then who/when/score. Do not start a yedi'a with weather or a slow scene unless it is a signed column.
3. **Put `(יום)` next to הערב/היום/אתמול.** It is the Sport5 dateline. Use it in match and news ledes.
4. **Israeli score order:** when A beats B, `ניצחה X:Y` with the winner's goals on the right (`1:2` = won 2–1). Keep that if the paper is speaking to Israeli-sports readers.
5. **One nickname per club, used as grammar.** האדומים ניצחו, דאגה ירוקה, במדי הפטישים — color words replace repetition of the full name after first mention.
6. **Minute codes are a dialect.** `בדקה ה-73`, `90+6`, `(10)` after a name. Goal grafs are one breath: time + how + temporary score.
7. **Stack quotes with a colon, don't decorate them.** `סילבס: "…"`. Save אמר/סיפר for the first cue. Don't invent טען where the site would use אמר.
8. **Match recap = result → hero → scene → other side → next game.** History callbacks (`א-לה שביט אלימלך`) are allowed in the hero graf; they are not a license to ramble.
9. **Explainers get subheads and a "נעשה סדר".** Money, calendar, eligibility, fit. End on what happens this week, not a vibe.
10. **Analysis asks a question, then answers with one number and one role.** `מה ב"ש מצאה במלדה?` → עומק → 5.9 ק"מ. Hedge on the page (`מוקדם לדעת`, `לא מופרך`).
11. **Signed columns may use אנחנו and commands; desk copy may not.** The fan column can say `להוציא את הצ'קים`. The מערכת brief cannot.
12. **Close on a next concrete thing.** MRI, flight to Cyprus, presentation at 13:00, פגרה. That is the Sport5 full stop.
13. **Headline formulas to copy:** quote after a colon; `אחרי N דקות:` + human beat; wordplay for features (`מגלוך לגלוכיניו`); `פרשנות:` kicker only on signed argument.
14. **Live-blog voice is a separate tool.** One-line minutes, caption jokes (`השעון מדבר ירושלמית`), scoreboard flips. Use only when the paper is running a running report, not in a morning rewrite.
15. **Boast the scoop once, dryly.** `כפי שפורסם לראשונה בערוץ הספורט` / `כפי שפרסמתי לראשונה` — then move. Haiton can steal the posture (we told you) without the channel name.
