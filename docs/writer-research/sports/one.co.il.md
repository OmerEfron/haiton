### 1. מכבי ת"א זכתה באלוף האלופים עם 1:3 על ב"ש
- url: https://www.one.co.il/Article/528261.html
- type: match report (ידיעת משחק / result write-up of a cup final)
- structure: Dek first: nicknames + key stats (הצהובים, שלושה בישולים, צמד, קפטן). Lede is context-then-result in one breath — season opener, day-of-week in parentheses, score, venue, historic note (פעם התשיעית). No dateline city line; the venue sits inside the lede. Body is chronological: opening storm → early goal → equalizer → disallowed goal → second-half finish, with minute markers. Photo captions interrupt as one-line scene labels. Ends by looking both ways: winners keep the trophy / losers leave it, plus next-stage calendar (אירופה, ליגת העל).
- terminology: Score as `1:3` with the winner as the grammatical subject (`ניצחה 1:3`). Club shorthand ת"א / ב"ש. Color nicknames הצהובים, הבחורים של קני מילר. Goal verbs: נתן את היתרון, כובש באלגנטיות, מצא את הרשת, סגר את הסיפור. Time: אחרי עשר דקות, בדקה ה-58, בדקה ה-72. Day stamp: הערב (חמישי).
- phrases:
  - "הכדורגל הישראלי חזר ובגדול"
  - "יריית הפתיחה של עונת 2026/27 נשמעה הערב (חמישי)"
  - "המשחק בבירת הנגב נפתח בסערה עם קצב מסחרר"
  - "כובש באלגנטיות עם העקב ומשווה ל-1:1"
  - "השער נפסל בטענה לנבדל"
  - "מצא את הרשת כבר בדקה ה-58"
  - "סגר את הסיפור עם צמד אישי"
  - "קוטפים כעת את התואר הראשון לעונת 2026/27"

- structure: Headline is a verdict-metaphor (`מתנות קטנות`) plus score. Dek names nicknames, scorers, and the temporary equalizer. Lede frames the competition (גביע הטוטו, מחזור ראשון) then drops the result. Graph 2 is pre-match posture (מחנה אימונים vs מאמן חדש על הקווים). - terminology: היהלומים / האדומים / הקבוצה האדומה מהבירה. Score as action: `עשה 0:1`, `עשה 1:3`. Error language: טעתה, שגה בענק, מתנה. Youth tag in parentheses: `(בן ה-16 בלבד)`. Time: בדקה העשירית, בדקה ה-24, בדקה ה-75.
- phrases:
  - "מתנות קטנות"
  - "היהלומים חגגו במשחק ה-1 בטוטו על האדומים"
  - "השוויון לא החזיק מים"

  - "גלגל לרשת חשופה ועשה 0:1"

- url: https://www.one.co.il/Article/511954.html
- type: interview (reported / roundup of a foreign-press sit-down, not live Q&A)
- structure: Headline = name + "בראיון" + punchy quote. Dek is a bullet-list of topics after a colon. Lede identifies the interview setting (בבלגיה), compresses the year, then drops the money quote in the same paragraph. Ends on the dream-club line (פרמייר ליג / ליברפול), not on the next fixture.
- terminology: הקיצוני הישראלי בן ה-21, האלופה (for Union), לדבריו. Age almost always rides next to the name. Attribution ladder: אמר / סיפר / הדגיש / הוסיף / התייחס / סיכם בזהירות. ONE does not claim the original sit-down; it is a Hebrew rewrite of an interview held abroad.
- phrases:
  - "ערך ראיון מקיף בבלגיה"
  - "ברור שאנחנו מסוגלים לנסוע ולנצח בבאיירן"

  - "גם כעת, לדבריו, זה המצב"
  - "על עתידו סיכם בזהירות"
  - "אמר בפשטות"

## Article types on this site
- **news / yedi'a**: Straight result or club fact. Headline = name + verb + place. Dek = numbers. Lede restates, then `כך נקבע אמש (יום)`. Short, few or no quotes. Recognize: gymnastics/world-event briefs, death notices, league notes.
- **match report / ידיעת משחק**: Headline has a score (`1:3`) and often a nickname or metaphor (`מתנות קטנות`). Dek lists scorers. Body is minute-by-minute. Closer is table, trophy, or next match. Recognize: `עשה 0:1`, `בדקה ה-72`, `סגר את הסיפור`.
- **preview**: Headline `מאמן: ציטוט`. Lede is the situation + `מחר (יום, שעה)`. Body is themed quote blocks. No result. Recognize: `דיבר לפני המשחק`, `לקראת המשחק`.
- **interview**: Headline is a quote, or `שם בראיון: ציטוט`. Two house forms: (a) live Q&A with the question as a bare line; (b) reported rewrite of a sit-down abroad, quote-plus-tag paragraphs. Recognize: `התפנה לדבר`, `ערך ראיון מקיף`, topic colon in the dek.
- **column / commentary / טור**: Labeled `טור מיוחד` in the dek or first graph. Expert byline. First person inside quotes. Subheds. Disclaimer at the end. Recognize: `לעניות דעתי`, `פרסם טור`.
- **locker-room / sourced club news**: Headline is a brutal anonymous line. Body uses `אמרו במועדון`, `טוענים`, chair-wobble metaphors, then the coach's on-record reply, then rumbles about רכש. Recognize: unnamed plural sources + pressure verbs.
- **transfer / רכש brief**: `חיזוק`, `על סף מעבר`, `חתם`, `הטרייד`. Deal grammar (השאלה, אופציה, סעיף מכירה). Scoop stamp `נחשף ב-ONE`. CV paragraph. Recognize: age-nationality-position epithet in the dek.
- **feature / magazine / profile**: Pun headline, correspondent byline (`שליח ONE`), childhood scenes, delayed quotes, lyric closer. Much longer than a yedi'a. Recognize: place names, family, "נפל לה האסימון".

## Recurring structure
- **Always a dek.** One to two lines under the H1. It is not optional. It packs nicknames, the one number that matters, and often a second name. Features use the dek as a topic menu + byline.
- **Lede rules.** News and match reports put the news in sentence one, then pin it with a time-stamp: `הערב (חמישי)`, `אמש (שישי)`, `היום (שני)`, `שלשום (רביעי)`. Match reports may open on a mood clause (`הכדורגל הישראלי חזר ובגדול`) but the score still lands in that same paragraph. Previews open on situation, not score. Features may open on the achievement, then immediately reverse into backstory. Interviews open by wrapping the occasion (signing, foreign sit-down) before the first quote.
- **Score in the first screen.** Match reports carry the score in the headline *and* the lede. Israeli order: when the subject won, write `ניצחה 1:3` (opponent first, winner second). In the running text a team "does" the score: `עשה 0:1`, `משווה ל-1:1`, `עשה 1:3`.
- **Paragraph rhythm.** Match reports: 4–6 medium graphs, one goal or one turning point each, photo caption as a beat. Yedi'ot: 2–3 short graphs. Transfers: lede + restatement + CV. Interviews: Q then A, or tag-sentence + quote + `הוסיף`. Columns: intro wrap, then "I" blocks under a subhed. Features: long scene graphs, quotes after the picture is set.
- **Where quotes sit.** Match reports: almost none (action does the talking). Previews/interviews: quotes are the body, grouped by topic, each with a verb (`אמר`, `סיפר`, `הדגיש`, `הוסיף`, `סיכם`). Locker-room pieces: the anonymous club line is the nut graf; the coach answers after. Transfers: optional player quote from the club site, plus `המועדון מסר/ה`. Features: quotes late, after biography.
- **Subheds.** Used in longer club stories and columns (`### "שופט גדול"`, `### "עוד לא סיימנו לבנות את הקבוצה"`). Rare in a 400-word match report.
- **How it ends.** Match report → both teams' next step (trophy, table, `בעוד ארבעה ימים תפגוש`). Preview → last quote. Interview Q&A → last answer, no moral. Reported interview → the dream or the caution (`סיכם בזהירות`). Transfer-not-done → CV stops. Transfer-done → official statement. Column → image + disclaimer. Feature → a visual motif returning (חיוך). Yedi'a → a one-line judgment (`עוד רחוקה מיכולת גבוהה`).
- **ONE as a character.** Scoops are stamped in the dek and again in graph 1–2: `שמועמדותו נחשפה ב-ONE`, `כפי שנחשף ב-ONE`, `חשיפת ONE`. Do not hide the desk.

## What a personal-newspaper writer should steal
1. **Write the dek before the body.** ONE never ships a sports piece without a standfirst that a skimmer can steal. Nickname + the number + one other name. If the piece is a column or interview, the dek is a topic list, not a teaser.
2. **Stamp the hour.** Almost every news lede has `הערב (יום)` or `אמש (יום)`. A personal paper can do the same with the reader's day: when it happened sits next to what happened.
3. **Israeli score grammar.** Subject won → `ניצחה 1:3`. In the running copy the team *does* the score (`עשה 0:1`) instead of "took a 1-0 lead". Keep that.
4. **One turning point per paragraph in a match report.** Open on season/context + score. Then a graph per goal or VAR moment, with a minute. Last graph is not a star rating — it is next match / what the trophy means for both sides.
5. **Headline is a verdict or a quote, not a label.** `מתנות קטנות:` is the story; the score is the proof. Interviews lead with the sentence the subject would put on a dressing-room wall.
6. **Nickname as grammar, not garnish.** הצהובים / האדומים / היהלומים / הירוקים replace the club name after first mention. Pair with a place metonym (בירת הנגב, הכרמל) once, not every sentence.
7. **Quote verbs are a ladder, not synonyms dumped at random.** First mention `אמר` or `דיבר`. Add detail with `סיפר`. Raise the volume with `הדגיש`. Extra sentence `הוסיף`. Close the door `סיכם` / `סיכם בזהירות`. Club voice is `מסרה` or `אמרו במועדון` — never give an unnamed source a name.
8. **Transfers are a status, a deal-shape, then a CV.** Lede = who + to whom + שאלה/רכישה/אופציה. Graph 2 = the scoop stamp if you have one. Then age, previous clubs, minutes, money. Stop. Do not invent "he will be a great signing".
9. **Previews are quote packets.** Situation lede, then `על X: "…"`, `לסיום דיבר על Y`. Do not write tactical essays the coach did not say.
10. **Features earn the lyric.** A magazine lede can scene-set, but only after (or as) the fact is on the table. Put the childhood graph in the middle. End on an image that was planted earlier (the smile, the girl in the corner) — not on a fixture.
11. **Keep ONE's honesty ticks.** Age beside the name (`בן ה-16 בלבד`, `בן ה-27`). Disallowed goals get `בטענה ל-`. Not-yet-signed deals stay `על סף` / `טרם הבשיל`. Expert columns carry `לעניות דעתי`. Steal the caution, not just the swagger.
12. **Length is a type, not a mood.** A world-championship yedi'a can be three graphs. A Super Cup match report is six. A profile can run because it has scenes. Do not inflate a Toto match into a feature, and do not crush an interview into a scoreline.
