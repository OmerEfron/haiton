# News-desk craft for Haiton (personal Hebrew newspaper)

Sources: `ynet.co.il.md` (7 full articles; 3 teasers/truncated), `n12.co.il.md` (11 full), `13tv.co.il.md` (8 full; 1 interview video-only). No invented body copy.

This summary is for a writer agent that writes **personal life** (work week, family dinner, apartment decision) in the **voice of Israeli news desks** — so a "news" piece, a "profile", a "feature", an "interview", and a "column" actually read as different genres. Steal structure and house grammar, not politics, crime, or TV metadata.

**Shared Israeli desk grammar (steal this):** weekday in parentheses is the dateline; the dek is mandatory and dense; news is inverted pyramid; quotes have a verb ladder and legal hedges; endings are typed by genre; city names appear as *scene*, not as a slug.

**Difference that matters for Haiton:** ynet gives **print discipline** (packed dek, present interviewer, homey interview close). N12 gives **edition energy** (bullet dek, reaction sandwich, brand-as-witness). 13tv gives **package length** (3–6 graphs, topic-map interview, Q&A only in magazine). Use ynet for news/interview bones, N12 for political-declaration and column punch, 13tv for how short a yedi'a is allowed to stay.

## Map to Haiton product types

### News (ידיעה)
Steal: inverted pyramid + `הבוקר (יום…)` + dek that already reports. Sentence one = who decided what, when, with what status (`לא חשודים`, `נאלצו לקבוע`). Graph 2 = the number or the named witness. Middle = one background dump (`נזכיר`). Close = the other side `מסרו` / a next concrete hour — never a moral.

Personal translation:
- Work: `הבוקר (שלישי) הודיעה דנה לצוות שהפרויקט נסגר — אחרי שלושה חודשים של דד-ליין.` Not a mood lede about the office light.
- Family: event + time before feeling (`אמש (שישי) ישבו שבעה סביב השולחן…`).
- Apartment: the decision and the number in sentence one (price / rooms / date keys are handed). Feeling is graph 3, attributed (`לדבריה`).

Do not open news on a scene. Do not close news on `וזה מלמד`. One quote as **proof**, not atmosphere.

### Profile
Steal N12 magazine (Weiss) + ynet sit-down (Shir / Rivlin): present-tense scene or archive date first, nut as a question (`איך…`), biography in the **middle** under a narrative subhed, gold quote late, close on a wager or a planted domestic line — not a CV.

Personal translation: open on who they are *this week* (the job, the kid at the door, the table they cook at). Origin story after the reader already cares. Age/role once (`דנה, עורכת בת ה-34`). Closer is an image already planted (grandkids at three, the smile), not "היא אישה מדהימה".

### Feature
Steal ynet's `עוד נחזור לרגע הזה` + N12's sensory open (mattress / cath lab) + 13tv magazine's labelled slices. Scene → nut (`מאחורי … עומדים`) → quote-subheds → horizon kicker. Color after the picture, not instead of a fact.

Personal translation: a weekend kitchen, a house hunt, a week that changed. Thesis in the dek. Three beats with subheds that sound like speech (`"החלטתי לקפוץ למים"`), not folder names (`רקע`). End on a horizon (`עד סוף החודש`) or a returned image.

Do not write a feature lede for a Tuesday status update. If there is a deadline today, it is news.

### Interview
Two house forms — pick one and stay in it:

1. **Print Q&A** (ynet Shir / 13tv Zuzu): short questions, long answers, interviewer audible (`שאלתי` / bold Q). Close on the last human line, no reporter sermon.
2. **Edition writeup** (13tv Eisenkot / N12 Troper video): hed = quote. Dek = four verbs that are the outline (`מסביר / מבהיר / מזהיר / תוקף`). Body has no printed questions (`בנוגע ל…`). Close on identity, not policy.

Personal translation: dinner-table and roommate interviews should be form 1. A "what she announced at work" piece can be form 2 (declaration → topic blocks → one reaction). Headline is the fridge-door sentence. Never mix Q&A with a columnist's `להערכתי`.

### Column
Steal the tag and the ending, not the swagger:
- Label it (`| פרשנות` or `| דעה`). Unsigned desk news may not say `אני`.
- Open on an aphorism or a false consensus (`אז למי להאמין? לאף אחד.` / `כשאתה מוציא אקדח מהמגירה…`), then convert to this week's fact.
- Few or no sourced quotes; the warrant is the byline.
- Close on advice (`להערכתי`), an aphorism, or `הפרק הבא` — never on `מסרו`.

Personal translation: the parent-at-the-table argument, the "we should not have signed that lease" piece, the fan-in-the-kitchen rant. First person is allowed because it is tagged. One demand. No inverted-pyramid fake-news opening.

## Headline craft (כותרת vs כותרת משנה)

**כותרת**
- News: verdict + the precise status (`לא חשודים ברצח`, not just `חקירה`). Or a command (`דנה לצוות:`).
- Interview: name + gold quote. Optionally `| ריאיון`.
- Column: thesis with a colon (`עליית מדרגה:`) or a slap that is already the conclusion.
- Profile/feature: nickname or paradox in quotes (`"אין עוד אחת כמוני"`), then who they are.
- Never a folder (`על העבודה`, `סופ״ש משפחתי`).

**כותרת משנה**
- Mandatory. 1–4 lines. Who + what + why now + (optional) the best quote.
- N12 trick: `•` between two facts, or a short quote after the pinpoint.
- 13tv interview trick: the dek is a **map** of the blocks that follow — if the dek lists four verbs, the body has four blocks.
- Do not repeat the headline word-for-word (13tv Tamari failure). Do not tease; report.

## Lede craft

- **News:** one sentence carrying actor + verb of decision + `הבוקר (יום X)` + the legal/status hedge. City only if it is the scene of a feature.
- **Interview writeup:** highlighted quote, then `תקף הערב (רביעי) בריאיון…`.
- **Print interview / feature:** scene or archive date. `הוא התעורר בביתו בירושלים.` / mattresses in an industrial zone. Nut in graph 2.
- **Column:** metaphor or rhetorical question; fact in sentence two.
- Load: news ledes are *overfull* on purpose. Feature ledes are *underfull* on purpose (picture first). Do not split the difference into a soft essay.

## Quote craft

Verb ladder (do not synonym-spin):
- Official / family institution: `מסר` / `מסרו` / `נמסר כי` / `בהודעה נמסר`
- Named person, attributed: `אמר`, `לדבריו`, `הבהיר`, `הוסיף`
- Conversation with the paper: `בשיחה איתי` (N12's `בשיחה עם N12`)
- Interview texture: `הודה`, `סיפר`, `מתוודה`, `שאלתי`
- Column: almost none; or reconstructed speech without a press quote

Placement:
- News: document or first responder in graph 2; other side closes the piece.
- Politics/announcement: quote early, then a **reaction sandwich** (rival, then ally softener).
- Interview: quotes *are* the body. Print form keeps the questions; TV form swallows them.
- Feature/profile: scene first, then speech; subheds can be the quotes themselves.
- Hedge beside heat: `על פי החשד` next to a hard hed; `לדבריה` next to a feeling. Strength and caution in the same sentence.

## Craft rules for the personal-life writer

1. **Dek before body.** Role + the one fact that matters + why this day. Interviews/columns: topic map, not a teaser.
2. **Stamp the weekday in parentheses.** `הבוקר (שלישי)` / `אמש (שישי)` / `בסביבות השעה 20:00`. That is the Israeli news metronome. No `ירושלים.` slug.
3. **Genre is a machine, not a mood.** News = pyramid + `מסרו` tail. Interview = quote body. Feature = scene then nut. Profile = present → middle bio → wager. Column = tagged "I" + aphorism close.
4. **Headline is a verdict or a fridge-quote.** Status-precise in news; colon-thesis in a column; never a topic label.
5. **One beat per paragraph.** Decision, number, quote, background dump, other side — in that order for news. Last graph matches the type.
6. **Quote ladder + hedge.** `נמסר` ≠ `לדבריו` ≠ `שאלתי`. Unfinished or disputed facts stay `על פי…` / `ככל הנראה` / `נציין`.
7. **Reaction sandwich on announcements.** After someone declares (new job, new lease, not coming to dinner), give the other person the tail — then maybe a softener. Do not end on the speech.
8. **Scene is a privilege of feature/interview.** `עוד נחזור לרגע הזה` is legal in a profile; it is a bug in a yedi'a.
9. **Interviewer audible only in interviews.** `שאלתי` / bold Q in magazine; `בנוגע ל` in a writeup. Desk news has no "I".
10. **Close on a typed kicker.** News: official line or next hour. Interview: kitchen sentence. Profile: bet or planted image. Feature: horizon. Column: advice/aphorism/`הפרק הבא`.
11. **Subheds are speech or a turn**, not `רקע`. `"הקירות רעדו"` / `"אז מה עכשיו?"` beat `פרטים נוספים`.
12. **Length is a type.** A yedi'a may be 3–6 graphs (13tv package). A profile runs because it has a middle. Do not inflate Tuesday into magazine, and do not crush a sit-down into a status line.

## Do not (the sameness failures)

- The same soft essay with a new hed: scene + feeling + "I" + lyric close, regardless of type.
- Weather / kitchen steam / "השמש זרחה" as a **news** lede.
- First person in untagged news. `אני` is a column/interview privilege.
- Dek that clones the headline, or a missing dek.
- Topic headlines (`על הדירה`, `שבוע בעבודה`) with no verdict and no quote.
- `טען` / `מסר` / `הודה` on a loop instead of the ladder.
- Quote in sentence one of news *instead of* the fact (unless the type is interview).
- Moral closer in desk voice (`וזה מלמד אותנו על המשפחה`). Morals live in signed columns, and even then prefer a demand.
- Biography dump in graph 1 of a profile (CV before we have a scene or a present-tense reason to care).
- Q&A mixed with `להערכתי` mixed with a lyric childhood in one untitled blob.
- TV furniture: `זמן צפייה`, `המהדורה המרכזית`, fake `בלעדי`. Personal paper can say `בשיחה איתי` once; it should not cosplay a channel.
- Random related-incident dump at the end of a death/breakup yedi'a (13tv fire → climate orgs). If the tail is not the other side or the next hour, cut it.
- Ending every piece on a homey last line. That close belongs to interviews. News that ends on grandkids is a failed type.

## Steal list, one line each (for the writer agent)

1. Dek is a second hed; interviews use it as a block map.
2. Fact + `(יום)` in the first breath of news.
3. Headline = verdict, command, or fridge-quote — not a folder.
4. Pyramid for news; scene for feature; quote-body for interview; tagged "I" for column.
5. Quote ladder; hedge beside heat; unnamed stays unnamed.
6. News quote = proof. Interview quote = body. Feature quote = after the picture. Column almost quote-free.
7. Announcement pieces get a reaction sandwich; they do not end on the speech.
8. Background dump after the nut, under a turn-subhed — not as the lede.
9. Kickers are typed: `מסרו` / last answer / wager-or-image / horizon / aphorism.
10. Present interviewer only when the piece is an interview.
11. 3–6 graphs is a complete yedi'a. Extra length must buy a scene or a middle.
12. If two pieces this week share the same lede shape, change the **genre machine** — not the adjectives.
