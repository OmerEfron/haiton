# Desk brief: Israeli house style → Haiton writer

Research (Aug 2026): ONE, Sport5, ynet, N12, חדשות 13. Full notes in `sports/` and `news/`. This page is what the writer agent must obey.

## The sameness failure

The live writer always ran as **intimate + feature**. Every piece opened like a magazine essay, stuffed the karteset into every graph, and closed on a homey moral. Israeli desks do not do that. Genre is a **machine**, not a mood.

## Five machines

| Type | Lede | Quotes | Close | Pronouns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **news** | Fact + `הבוקר (יום)` in sentence one | One quote as proof | Other side `מסרו` or next hour | No `אני` |
| **profile** | Who they are *this week* | After the picture | Wager or planted image | Third person |
| **feature** | Scene, then nut | After the scene; subheds can be speech | Horizon or returned image | Third; scene is legal |
| **interview** | Fridge-quote hed; dek is a topic map | Quotes *are* the body (Q&A or writeup) | Last human line | Interviewer only here |
| **column** | Aphorism or false consensus | Almost none | Demand / `הפרק הבא` | Tagged `אני` only |

If two pieces would share a lede shape, **change the machine**, not the adjectives.

## Shared Israeli grammar

- Dek is a second hed. Never skip it. Never clone the headline.
- Dateline is a weekday: `הערב (שלישי)`, not `חיפה.`
- Headline = verdict, command, or fridge-quote — never a folder (`על העבודה`).
- One beat per paragraph. Length is a type (3–6 graphs can be a complete yedi'a).
- Quote ladder: `אמר` → `סיפר` → `הדגיש` → `הוסיף` → `סיכם`. Institution: `מסרו`. Do not synonym-spin `טען`.
- Karteset is a **check**, not stuffing. Unused biography stays out.
- Hedge unfinished facts (`על סף`, `לדבריה`, `מוקדם לדעת`).
- Endings are typed. Homey last lines belong to interviews, not news.

## Pick the machine from the interview

- Discrete event with when/what → **news** (default if unclear but there is a when)
- The answers *are* the story → **interview**
- The person is the story → **profile**
- A stretch of life / scene / phenomenon → **feature**
- They took a side → **column**

Do not pick feature because it sounds writerly.
